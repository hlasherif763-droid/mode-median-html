Concept 1

What is the MODE?

Most Common

The MODE is the number or item that appears the MOST OFTEN in a data set!

Book Tip!

Animals: cat, cat, mouse, rabbit.

Cat is the Mode!

Memory Trick

Concept 2

What is the MEDIAN?

The MEDIAN is the middle number when data is written in order!

W1

Original Heights: 133 cm, 137 cm, 134 cm, 131 cm, 132 cm

Step 1: Write the numbers in order from smallest to greatest.

Real-Life Motivation

Why Do We Need Averages in Real Life?

Averages help us solve everyday mysteries, make smart shopping choices, and understand what's popular!

Book Mystery #1

The Seed Height Mystery

Two seed packets list average heights: Packet A (1 m) and Packet B (2 m). Look at the garden below! Which packet belongs to which flower?

Dark Blue (1m)
Yellow (2m)
🌿 Dark Blue seeds = Packet with 1m average! Yellow seeds = Packet with 2m average!
Book Mystery #2

The Movie Review Score

"The Fun Film" has an Average Score of 6 out of 10. Does this mean everyone gave it 6?

Viewer 1: 1/10 (Hated it) 1/10
Viewer 2: 10/10 (Loved it!) 10/10
Viewer 3: 7/10 (Good) 7/10
🍿 Averages collect everyone's opinions into ONE helpful number so you can decide!
